# Privacy Policy ## Max's eBay Management **Last updated: 31 October 2026** ### 1. About this application Max's eBay Management is a privately operated, personal-use application developed to manage and analyse the owner's own eBay seller account. The application is not publicly distributed, does not provide services to other users and is not operated as a commercial application or third-party data service. ### 2. Information accessed With the account owner's authorisation, the application accesses information through the official eBay Developer API. This may include: - eBay listing information. - Product descriptions, prices and quantities. - Order and transaction information. - Sales performance and analytics. - Inventory and fulfilment information. - Buyer information contained within order records, where required for order management. ### 3. How information is used Information is used exclusively by the application owner for: - Managing personal eBay listings. - Creating and updating product listings. - Reviewing sales performance. - Analysing inventory and stock levels. - Managing orders and fulfilment. - Producing internal business reports. Information is not sold, rented, licensed or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. ### 4. Data storage and security The application is designed for private use on the owner's own computer. Application data and downloaded reports are stored locally. OAuth credentials and access tokens are treated as confidential and protected against unauthorised access. No buyer information is intentionally transmitted to external analytics or advertising services. ### 5. Data retention and deletion Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the application's management and reporting functions. The application owner can delete locally stored information and revoke the application's access to their eBay account. ### 6. Third-party services The application communicates with eBay through its official Developer APIs. Use of eBay services is subject to eBay's applicable terms and privacy policies. ### 7.
